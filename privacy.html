How information is handled when you visit the Zovariq Digital website or contact our team.

1. Who we are

Zovariq Digital is the trading name used on this website. For privacy enquiries, contact tahawarabbas76@gmail.com. The name and address of the legal entity or sole trader responsible for processing personal data must be confirmed before this notice is published.

2. Information we receive

When you choose to send an enquiry, you may provide your name, work email address, company or brand, requested service, and project details. If you contact us via WhatsApp, email, or the linked booking calendar, we may receive the information you share through those services, including appointment details and correspondence.

The enquiry form on this website does not send your answers to a Zovariq Digital server. It prepares a message in your WhatsApp or email application. Your message is sent only if you complete the send action in that application. Please avoid submitting confidential or sensitive information in an initial enquiry.

3. Why we use it and lawful bases

We use enquiry information to respond, assess a proposed project, arrange discussions, provide quotations, and manage any resulting business relationship. Depending on the circumstances, the lawful basis may be steps taken at your request before a contract or our legitimate interests in handling relevant business communications, where those interests are not overridden by your rights. We do not rely on consent merely because you have made a business enquiry.

Any separate direct marketing must have an appropriate lawful basis and comply with applicable electronic marketing rules. You can object to direct marketing at any time.

4. Providers and recipients

External providers may process data when you use their services: GitHub Pages hosts this website; WhatsApp (Meta) or your chosen email provider handles messages you send; and Google Calendar handles bookings made using the linked appointment page. These providers may process account, usage or technical data under their own privacy notices. Other service providers should be listed here if subsequently introduced.

5. International handling

Service providers and recipients may operate in countries other than yours. Before publication, we must document where relevant personal information is stored or accessed and the legal mechanism for any restricted transfers where UK or EU data-protection rules apply.

6. Retention and security

We intend to keep enquiry correspondence only for as long as needed to respond, consider a project, comply with applicable obligations, or resolve a dispute. The specific retention periods or defensible retention criteria for enquiries and client records must be confirmed before this policy is published. Appropriate access restrictions and account security measures should be maintained for communication services.

7. Cookies and analytics

As of this draft review, the main website code does not include a Zovariq Digital analytics tracker or marketing-cookie script. External sites reached from our links may use their own cookies or tracking. We will review this section before adding analytics, advertising pixels, embedded forms or similar services.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, or request portability of your personal information. You may withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. To make a request, contact tahawarabbas76@gmail.com. If UK data-protection law applies, you may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

9. Updates

We will update this notice when our services, data practices, third-party providers or applicable legal requirements change.
