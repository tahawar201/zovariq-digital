Website Privacy & Enquiry Notice

What happens when you browse this website or choose to contact us, and how to ask about personal information you have shared.

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Applies to this website and initial business enquiries

Who we are and how to contact us

Zovariq Digital is an independent digital-services brand operating from Jhang, Punjab, Pakistan. For privacy enquiries, corrections or deletion requests, contact tahawarabbas76@gmail.com. This notice does not claim that Zovariq Digital is a UK-registered company.

How this website's enquiry form works

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. The project enquiry form runs in your browser: it prepares a message for WhatsApp or your email application. You choose whether to send it. The website's own form code does not automatically submit your message to a Zovariq server or store a copy in a website database.

The fields you may choose to provide are your name, work email, business or brand name, service interest and project details. Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, health records or other sensitive information in an initial enquiry.

Why we use information you send

If you send an enquiry, we use the information you provide to read and respond to your request, discuss possible services, prepare a requested proposal and manage any related correspondence. Sending an enquiry does not, by itself, subscribe you to a marketing list.

Where UK data-protection law applies, responding to a requested business enquiry may rely on legitimate interests in handling that contact or steps requested before entering a contract, depending on the circumstances. Other activities, including marketing or client-project processing, require their own assessment and appropriate basis.

Hosting and external services

  • GitHub Pages: delivers the website and may process technical connection information as the hosting provider. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • WhatsApp: if you choose the WhatsApp option and send your message, the conversation is handled through WhatsApp and your device. See WhatsApp's privacy information.
  • Email: if you choose email, your email application and the relevant mail providers handle the message and its delivery.
  • Google Calendar: the consultation-booking link opens an external Google scheduling service, which has its own data-handling terms. See Google's privacy policy.

These services may process information in countries outside your own. This notice cannot replace their terms or a project-specific data-processing agreement.

Cookies and website analytics

The website code reviewed for this notice does not contain a first-party advertising pixel or analytics tracking script. This does not mean that the hosting provider or external services never use cookies or technical logs. If tracking or embedded third-party features are introduced, this notice and any required consent mechanism should be reviewed before use.

How long messages are kept

The website's form does not maintain a submission database. If you choose to send a message, copies may remain in the relevant email or messaging accounts while needed to answer your enquiry, follow up on requested work, maintain relevant business records or meet applicable obligations. The appropriate retention period depends on the correspondence and any resulting engagement; no universal deletion deadline is represented here. Contact us if you want information about a specific conversation or to request deletion.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can decide not to send the prepared message. You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold from your correspondence, to request correction or deletion, or to raise a concern. Rights and exceptions depend on the law that applies to your circumstances. Where UK data-protection law applies, you can also consult the UK Information Commissioner's Office for guidance and complaints.

We may need enough information to verify that a request relates to you before acting on it. Please use the email address above for privacy questions.

Scope and updates

This notice describes the website and initial enquiry channels. It does not describe a client's future AI receptionist, CRM, recorded calls, mailing lists or other project systems; those require separate scope, provider and privacy reviews before handling live customer information. We will update this page when our website's actual data-handling arrangements materially change.
