RELIABILITY & CONTROL

What we check before launch

We agree approved knowledge sources, conversation boundaries, escalation triggers, data handling expectations, channel permissions and test cases. AI responses can be wrong, so sensitive decisions stay with qualified people. Integrations, third-party fees, launch timing and ongoing maintenance are confirmed in the project scope.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can the AI answer every question?

No. We define its knowledge and boundaries. It should escalate unfamiliar or sensitive questions rather than guess.

Can it work with my current tools?

Potentially. We first check integrations, permissions, subscription costs and technical feasibility.

Will a person remain involved?

Yes. Human review, handoff and approval are designed into the workflow.

Can it work with WhatsApp or social messaging?

Possibly, using supported official platform APIs and approved accounts. Availability and restrictions vary by channel and are checked during discovery.

How much does it cost and how long will it take?

We provide a scoped proposal after understanding your channels, integrations, knowledge content and support needs. Third-party subscription and usage charges are identified separately.
