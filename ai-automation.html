BEFORE YOU CHOOSE A PROVIDER

AI receptionist buyer checklist for service businesses

A useful AI receptionist project starts with the enquiries your team actually receives, not a promise that every call or message can be automated. Use these questions to compare suppliers and prepare a practical project brief.

A

Channels and coverage

Decide whether you need website chat, telephone calls, out-of-hours requests or a combination. Ask which channels are genuinely supported, what the usage limits are and what happens when a provider is unavailable.

B

Answers and escalation

Identify who approves the knowledge base, how outdated answers are corrected and when a caller reaches a person. Require a fallback for unclear, urgent or sensitive enquiries instead of confident guesses.

C

Booking and lead capture

Separate a request from a confirmed appointment. A booking is confirmed only when an authorised calendar or business system records it successfully; otherwise the assistant should state that staff will review the request.

D

Privacy, costs and ownership

Ask where messages and recordings are processed, who can access them, how long they are kept and how deletion works. Review subscription, phone, integration, support and exit costs before approving a proposal.

For UK organisations, review the Information Commissioner's Office guidance on lawful, fair and transparent data processing. The right requirements depend on the actual workflow; this checklist is not legal advice.

TEST BEFORE LAUNCH

Four acceptance tests for an AI receptionist pilot

Ask the provider to demonstrate these cases with test data and your approved business rules before connecting live customer enquiries.

01

Routine enquiry

Ask about opening hours, services and a common policy. Check that answers match approved information and that the assistant admits when it does not know.

02

Unclear or sensitive request

Try an ambiguous question and a request outside the permitted scope. Check that the assistant does not invent advice and can offer an appropriate human route.

03

Failed integration

Simulate an unavailable calendar, inbox or phone transfer. Confirm the caller receives an accurate fallback, not a false booking or delivery confirmation.

04

Verified handover

Check that a test enquiry reaches the authorised team with only necessary details, that the team can act on it and that access and retention rules are documented.

Agree how success will be measured: correctly answered routine questions, verified handovers, confirmed bookings, error reports and staff review time. COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can the AI answer every question?

No. We define its knowledge and boundaries. It should escalate unfamiliar or sensitive questions rather than guess.

Can it work with my current tools?

Potentially. We first check integrations, permissions, subscription costs and technical feasibility.

Will a person remain involved?

Yes. Human review, handoff and approval are designed into the workflow.

Can it work with WhatsApp or social messaging?

Possibly, using supported official platform APIs and approved accounts. Availability and restrictions vary by channel and are checked during discovery.

How much does it cost and how long will it take?

We provide a scoped proposal after understanding your channels, integrations, knowledge content and support needs. Third-party subscription and usage charges are identified separately.
