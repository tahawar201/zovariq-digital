THE PROCESS

How it works, step by step.

Intelligent operations, with people in control. We tailor each stage to your requirements, available tools and approved project scope.

01

Discover the workflow

We learn how enquiries arrive, which questions repeat, where information is stored and when a human needs to take over.

02

Design the conversation

We outline the assistant's responsibilities, tone, knowledge sources, lead fields and escalation rules.

03

Build and connect

We configure the assistant and connect agreed tools such as website chat, booking calendars, email notifications or a CRM where supported.

04

Test before launch

We check common questions, missing information, unexpected requests, privacy considerations and handoff to your team.

05

Review and improve

We inspect approved conversation samples, refine answers and adjust workflows as your business needs evolve.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can the AI answer every question?

No. We define its knowledge and boundaries. It should escalate unfamiliar or sensitive questions rather than guess.

Can it work with my current tools?

Potentially. We first check integrations, permissions, subscription costs and technical feasibility.

Will a person remain involved?

Yes. Human review, handoff and approval are designed into the workflow.
